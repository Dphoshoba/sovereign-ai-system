# Gamma 2.0 Phase XXIII - Executive Intelligence

## Objective
Phase XXIII evolves Mission Control into an executive operating system.

Gamma answers:

- What is blocked?
- What is overdue?
- What creates the biggest ROI?
- Where are the risks?
- What should happen today?

## Canonical Contract
Implementation:

- `src/lib/gamma-2/executive-intelligence.ts`

Validation:

- `tests/gamma-2/executive-intelligence.test.ts`

## Executive Rule
Executive Intelligence is answer-first. It prioritizes governed action lists over raw dashboards.

## Boundary
Phase XXIII ranks and explains operational priorities. It does not execute work, publish changes, or bypass human owners.
