# EVDP Decision EVDP-2026-001

**Programme:** Echoes & Visions Deployment Programme (EVDP)
**Date:** 2026-07-20
**Decision:** Internal AI Organization Deployment
**Status:** AUTHORIZED

## Authorization

EVDP-001 — Internal AI Organization Deployment is authorized for execution.

## Mission

Deploy Gamma OS to operate Echoes & Visions itself before expanding to customers or additional products. Use Gamma OS to run Echoes & Visions before asking it to run anything else.

| ID | Workstream | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Executive Office | Briefings, strategy, meetings, priorities, risk, opportunities |
| 2 | Research Office | Evidence collection, cross-product reuse |
| 3 | Product Office | Roadmaps, capabilities, releases, prioritization, dependencies |
| 4 | Operations Office | Reviews, projects, documentation, deployment, compliance |
| 5 | Knowledge Office | Governed knowledge base, discoverability, versioning |

## Year One Success Criteria

By end of Year 1, Echoes & Visions should demonstrate:

1. Gamma OS supports real internal operations.
2. Leadership relies on AI-assisted operational intelligence.
3. Research is shared effectively across products.
4. Product planning is coordinated through a common operating model.
5. Organizational knowledge is governed and easy to access.
6. Operational decisions become increasingly data-informed.
