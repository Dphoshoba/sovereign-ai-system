## Description

Tracks governance compliance across all workstreams. Uses Federated Governance (8C) for policy domain evaluations and Federated Observability (8D) for compliance telemetry. Governed by O-004: compliance violations must be acknowledged within 7 days.

## Capabilities

- operations.compliance — compliance dashboard, policy violation trends, remediation recommendations
